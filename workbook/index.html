For parents

Your love don't cost a thing.

It's tempting to motivate kids with toys and candy. But research links heavy reliance on material rewards to more materialism later in life. Kids remember experiences, not stuff.

Try rewards like these instead:

  • Quality time: extra reading, a board game, a cuddle.
  • Special privileges: pick the movie or stay up a little later.
  • Experiences: the park, the zoo, a beach day.
  • Making things together: bake cookies, plant a garden, draw a picture.

The cotton ball jar

  1. Pick a family goal together: a zoo trip, a new trail, the ice cream shop.
  2. Every time your child helps without being asked, listens the first time, or works on the skill you're practicing, they add a cotton ball to the jar.
  3. When the jar is full, go celebrate!

It rewards good habits and teaches kids to work toward a goal. Pair it with the free goal chart and reward tickets above.
