Creative Direction · Brand Transformation · 2026

Building a brand system where strategy, storytelling, and technology work as one.

I led the visual rebrand and multi-channel content ecosystem for TrialSupport.us, transforming a company with no clearly defined visual identity into a cohesive, recognizable brand built for high-stakes legal and courtroom environments.

Creative Direction Brand Strategy Art Direction Motion Graphics 3D + AI Content Strategy Digital Marketing

01 · The Rebrand

From no discernible identity to a distinctive visual language.

TrialSupport.us began with an open-ended branding challenge. Two managing partners had different creative instincts: one favored a strict black-and-white system, while the other wanted a more experimental approach that could break traditional design boundaries. I used that tension as an opportunity to define a stronger strategic direction rather than simply split the difference.

02 · Brand Strategy

Choosing a visual position instead of following the obvious path.

Black-and-white branding had already been executed at an exceptional level by global brands such as Sephora and Adidas. Rather than imitate an established visual convention, I introduced a deep navy foundation and evolved the palette into a richer gem-tone system. The result gave TrialSupport a more ownable, contemporary presence while leaving room for expressive campaign art.

03 · AI + 3D Production

Building believable worlds instead of relying on generic stock imagery.

I created realistic TrialSupport-equipped courtroom and office environments in Blender, then used AI-assisted character generation and compositing to populate those environments with recurring visual personalities. This hybrid workflow combined the control of 3D production with the speed and creative range of AI.

04 · Legal Storytelling

Turning complex courtroom technology into clear visual narratives.

Beyond marketing, I worked directly with lawyers to create opening and closing presentation graphics, accident recreations, 3D visualizations, and courtroom demonstratives. The goal was always the same: take complex information and make it immediate, credible, and persuasive for an audience that may only have seconds to understand it.

05 · Content Ecosystem

Designing the campaign as a system—not a collection of disconnected assets.

I connected social media, website content, Google Ads insights, blog strategy, email marketing, and downloadable resources into one coordinated content engine. Analytics informed what topics and visuals were working, allowing the creative system to evolve around audience behavior rather than assumptions.

06 · Brand Across Touchpoints

One identity, built to perform everywhere.

The brand extended from social posts and motion graphics into brochures, email campaigns, web pages, downloadable guides, flyers, and courtroom presentations. Every touchpoint was designed to feel like part of the same organization while being purpose-built for its audience and channel.

07 · Personalized Marketing

Using personalization to make a B2B campaign feel one-to-one.

I developed both client and non-client eblast campaigns using merge tags to personalize messaging by city. Blog content and downloadable assets gave the campaigns a useful destination while social and email worked together to drive qualified traffic back to the site.

08 · AI With Art Direction

Using AI for speed without surrendering brand control.

AI became a production accelerator, not a replacement for design thinking. I used generative imagery to explore concepts and create campaign visuals, then applied human-led art direction, compositing, 3D environments, typography, logo placement, and brand standards to make the final work feel intentional and consistent.

The Impact

Creative direction backed by audience behavior, not just aesthetics.

The result was a repeatable creative and content system that could support brand awareness, education, lead generation, courtroom storytelling, and business development across channels. By combining brand strategy, motion, 3D, AI, UX-minded content design, and performance insights, I helped turn TrialSupport.us from a visually undefined company into a more recognizable and scalable brand experience.
