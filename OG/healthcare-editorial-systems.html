Healthcare Editorial Systems

Design systems built for clarity, care, and trust.

This collection showcases a comprehensive series of branded patient materials for Torrance Memorial Medical Center and MemorialCare, spanning flyers, guidebooks, seasonal publications, and custom illustration. The goal was to elevate every touchpoint with cohesive visual systems, clear information hierarchy, and thoughtful adaptations for diverse audiences.

Editorial Design Healthcare Branding Information Design Illustration Patient Education

Torrance Memorial Medical Center

Turning complex clinical information into clear, approachable patient experiences.

For Torrance Memorial Medical Center, I developed patient-facing editorial systems that combined clear information hierarchy, custom illustration, 3D visualization, wayfinding, and branded publication design. The goal was to make highly technical healthcare information easier to understand without sacrificing visual sophistication.

Joint Replacement Patient Guide

A second system built around confidence and clarity.

The joint replacement guide extended the same editorial principles into a distinct visual system, combining patient education, anatomical illustration, timelines, FAQs, and procedural guidance into a clear, reassuring experience.

A scalable editorial system

Consistency across every patient-facing touchpoint.

The work needed to feel unified without becoming repetitive. Flexible typography, modular page structures, coordinated color systems, and reusable illustration styles made it possible to adapt the brand across different service lines while keeping the experience recognizable and easy to navigate.

Patient guidebooks

Complex information made calm and approachable.

Guidebooks were designed to help patients move through unfamiliar medical experiences with greater confidence. Clear hierarchy, generous spacing, supportive photography, diagrams, and consistent navigation reduced visual friction while preserving a premium brand experience.

Modular campaign collateral

One system, many audiences.

Flyers, event materials, service-line campaigns, and patient education pieces were designed from a common visual foundation, then adjusted for tone, audience, and clinical context. The result was a system that felt coordinated at scale without sacrificing relevance.

Torrance Memorial Medical Center

Editorial storytelling supported by custom 3D illustration.

For orthopedic and joint-care materials, I developed a visual system that paired editorial layouts with custom anatomical imagery. The illustrations created a clearer bridge between clinical information and patient understanding while giving the brand a distinctive visual voice.

Campaign extensions

Flexible enough for wellness, rehabilitation, events, and education.

The same design discipline extended into branded flyers and program materials, allowing each topic to feel distinct while maintaining the trust, clarity, and consistency expected from a healthcare system.

Outcome

A cohesive editorial language that makes healthcare information easier to understand.

Across publications, patient guides, flyers, illustrations, and digital touchpoints, the system balanced brand consistency with empathy and usability — creating materials that felt polished, accessible, and appropriate for the people relying on them.
