Learning System

Built to grow with the learner.

Purchase the workbook on Amazon

Six full exercise series introduce skills through a gradually sloping difficulty curve. Word searches, vowel mazes, early phonics, tracing, and writing practice build muscle memory and manual dexterity while keeping the experience playful. Each book also introduces Spanish or French, giving young learners an early reason to explore beyond the immediate lesson.

Progressive Learning

Word searches that grow with the child. Vowel mazes where every path is the right path.

The system is built to support accomplishment rather than frustration. Activities become more challenging over time while giving children repeated opportunities to practice letterforms, vocabulary, pattern recognition, and foundational phonics.

Campaign Extensions

Learning content extended into a playful 3D brand world.

Seasonal content gave the educational system room to evolve beyond the workbook. I used 3D environments, illustrated challenges, and campaign messaging to create a recognizable world that could work across social, promotions, and digital touchpoints.

Seasonal Storytelling · Halloween

A familiar seasonal hook turned into a learning challenge.

For Halloween, the workbook world shifted into a more atmospheric environment centered around the pumpkin maze. The creative still worked as a clear promotional asset, while the vertical motion piece was built specifically for social viewing.

Separating the static campaign art from the motion experience kept the page from feeling like a video gallery and instead showed how one idea was adapted across formats.

Seasonal Storytelling · Valentine

A campaign that made the learning product part of the celebration.

The Valentine campaign translated the educational system into a softer, playful world without losing the brand’s core visual language. The still campaign focused on the idea that meaningful educational support does not need to feel expensive or overly serious.

Motion then brought the environment to life, giving the social piece its own pacing and personality while staying grounded in the same workbook-centered system.

Outcome

A learning product designed as a complete visual ecosystem.

M1ndseye grew from worksheets originally created for children into a broader educational system built around accessible learning, progressive skill development, illustration, 3D storytelling, motion, and printable tools. The goal remained consistent throughout: make learning more approachable, engaging, and rewarding for both children and parents.
